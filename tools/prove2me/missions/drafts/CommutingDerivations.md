## Motivation

A polynomial $f$ in $n$ complex variables is a **coordinate** when it is the image of a variable under a polynomial automorphism of $\mathbb{C}[z_1,\dots,z_n]$. The **Abhyankar–Sathaye conjecture** asks whether $\mathbb{C}[z_1,\dots,z_n]/(f)\cong\mathbb{C}^{[n-1]}$ forces $f$ to be a coordinate (OpenAI 2026, §1; Kraft 1996, §4). A **locally nilpotent derivation** is a derivation some iterate of which annihilates each fixed element; such derivations are the algebraic form of additive group actions. Maubach (2003) formulated the **commuting derivations conjecture** for $n-1$ commuting locally nilpotent derivations of $k^{[n]}$ that are linearly independent over $k^{[n]}$, asking whether their common kernel is generated by a coordinate, and proved affirmative four-variable results for equations $a(x)y+b(x,z,t)$ (Maubach 2003, Cor. 4.2–4.3, as cited in OpenAI 2026, §1).

The OpenAI preprint constructs an explicit $F\in\mathbb{C}[h,u,v,w]$ with $\mathbb{C}[h,u,v,w]/(F)\cong\mathbb{C}^{[3]}$ that is not a coordinate (Thm. 1.1, Cor. 1.2); that is row 1 of this family. The formalization docs page describes a companion statement about the same $F$: $n-1$ commuting, locally nilpotent derivations of $\mathbb{C}[X_0,\dots,X_{n-1}]$, linearly independent over the polynomial ring, with common kernel $\mathbb{C}[F]$ containing no ambient coordinate, the extra $n-4$ derivations being partial derivatives in the added variables. This row is that statement. Read against Maubach's formulation, it is a counterexample to the commuting derivations conjecture for every $n\ge4$.

*Timeline.* Abhyankar–Moh (1975) and Suzuki (1974) settled $n=2$ affirmatively. Sathaye (1976) and Russell (1976) rectified planes linear in one of three variables. Maubach (2000) computed kernels of triangular monomial derivations on $k[X_1,\dots,X_4]$, where the polynomials of this row arise (OpenAI 2026, §1). Shpilrain–Yu (2001) used the ambient critical locus to separate inequivalent embeddings. Kaliman–Vénéreau–Zaidenberg (2004) and Maubach (2003) straightened four-variable hypersurfaces $a(x)y+b(x,z,t)=0$ with affine-three-space zero fibre. The OpenAI preprint (2026) gives the four-variable counterexample and its extension to all $n\ge4$; the three-variable case is not addressed.

## Setting

Fix an integer $n\ge4$ (Lean `hn : 4 ≤ n`) and let $A_n=\mathbb{C}[X_0,\dots,X_{n-1}]$, in Lean `MvPolynomial (Fin n) ℂ`. In $R=A_4$ write $h=X_0$, $u=X_1$, $v=X_2$, $w=X_3$ and set
$$x=u^3+hv,\qquad y=-u^2+hw,\qquad s=S(h,u,v,w):=2u^3v+3u^4w+h\,(v^2-3u^2w^2)+h^2w^3,$$
$$p=P(x,y,s):=-2s^2x+3sy^2-3s^3y,\qquad F=h-p-1 .$$
Here $S$ and $P$ are **universal polynomials** over every commutative ring; the construction perturbs the cusp parametrization $(u^3,-u^2)$ by multiples of $h$, and $x^2+y^3=hs$ in $R$ (OpenAI 2026, (1.1)–(3.1)). The **extended polynomial** $F_n\in A_n$ (`extendedF hn`) is $F$ read in the first four variables through `fourInclusion hn : Fin 4 → Fin n`; so $F_4=F$, and $F_n$ does not involve $X_4,\dots,X_{n-1}$.

A **$\mathbb{C}$-derivation** of $A_n$ is a $\mathbb{C}$-linear map $d:A_n\to A_n$ with $d(ab)=a\,d(b)+b\,d(a)$ (`Derivation ℂ (MvPolynomial (Fin n) ℂ) (MvPolynomial (Fin n) ℂ)`); it annihilates constants. Derivations form an $A_n$-module under $(a\cdot d)(b)=a\,d(b)$. A derivation is **locally nilpotent** (`LocallyNilpotent`) if for every $a\in A_n$ some iterate $d^{m}(a)$ vanishes, with $m\in\mathbb{N}$ depending on $a$. Two derivations **commute** when $d\circ d'=d'\circ d$ as maps. A family $(d_i)$ is **linearly independent over $A_n$** (`LinearIndependent (MvPolynomial (Fin n) ℂ) d`) when $\sum_i a_i\cdot d_i=0$ with $a_i\in A_n$ forces all $a_i=0$. The **common kernel** is $\{a\in A_n: d_i(a)=0\text{ for all }i\}$. $\mathbb{C}[F_n]$ is the $\mathbb{C}$-subalgebra generated by $F_n$ (`Algebra.adjoin ℂ {extendedF hn}`), the set of $q(F_n)$ with $q\in\mathbb{C}[t]$. An element $g$ is a coordinate of $A_n$ if some $\mathbb{C}$-algebra automorphism $e$ (`≃ₐ[ℂ]`) and index $j$ satisfy $e(X_j)=g$. $\partial_j$ (`pderiv j`) is the **partial derivative** with respect to $X_j$.

**Indexing.** `blocksToAmbient hn : Fin (n-4) ⊕ Fin 4 ≃ Fin n` puts the distinguished block first: the four distinguished indices go to $0,\dots,3$ and the $i$-th extra index to $4+i$. `blocksToOutput hn : Fin (n-4) ⊕ Fin 3 ≃ Fin (n-1)` sends the three distinguished indices to $0,1,2$ and the $i$-th extra index to $3+i$.

## Formalization targets

### Goal

For every integer $n\ge4$ there exists a family $d=(d_0,\dots,d_{n-2})$ of $\mathbb{C}$-derivations of $A_n$ such that
$$\begin{aligned}
&\text{(i)}\ \ d_i(F_n+1)=0\ \text{ for all } i; \qquad \text{(ii)}\ \ d_i\circ d_j=d_j\circ d_i\ \text{ for all } i,j;\\
&\text{(iii)}\ \text{each } d_i \text{ is locally nilpotent}; \qquad \text{(iv)}\ \ d \text{ is linearly independent over } A_n;\\
&\text{(v)}\ \ \{a\in A_n : d_i(a)=0 \text{ for all } i\}=\mathbb{C}[F_n];\\
&\text{(vi)}\ \ \text{no element } g \text{ of that common kernel is a coordinate of } A_n;\\
&\text{(vii)}\ \ d_{3+i}=\partial_{4+i}\ \text{ for } 0\le i<n-4 .
\end{aligned}$$

Since derivations annihilate $1$, (i) says $d_i(F_n)=0$. Left unfixed: the derivations $d_0,d_1,d_2$ themselves (any triple meeting (i)–(vi) with the prescribed extras is acceptable) and the nilpotency exponents. Conjunct (vi) ranges over every element of the kernel, including constants and $F_n$. Nothing is asserted for $n\le3$ or about the quotient $A_n/(F_n)$.

## Significance

The theorem combines, for one explicit polynomial, $n-1$ commuting linearly independent locally nilpotent derivations, the exact kernel $\mathbb{C}[F_n]$, and the absence of any coordinate in it: an explicit negative instance, in every dimension at least four, of the commuting derivations conjecture as formulated by Maubach (2003).

Status, plainly: the listed preprint proves on paper the quotient isomorphism and the critical-point obstruction for $F$ (Theorem 1.1, Corollary 1.2). It does not state or prove the derivation statement of this row; its introduction only notes that the triangular derivation $D=h\partial_u-3u^2\partial_v+2u\partial_w$ annihilates $h,x,y,s$ and hence $F$. The commuting-derivations statement is recorded on the docs page as a companion. In Lean it is admitted with `sorry`; nothing in this row is machine-checked. Formalizing it would also create a locally-nilpotent-derivation toolkit (iterates, commuting families, kernels) that Mathlib lacks.

## Difficulty

The newcomer's first idea is partial derivatives. Three of $\partial_0,\dots,\partial_3$ commute, are locally nilpotent and linearly independent, but their common kernel is $\mathbb{C}[X_j]$ for the omitted variable, generated by a coordinate, and none annihilates $F$, which involves all four variables. The second idea uses row 1: since $R/(F)\cong\mathbb{C}[X,Y,T]$, lift $\partial_X,\partial_Y,\partial_T$ from the zero fibre to $R$. This fails for the reason the preprint stresses: an isomorphism of the zero fibre alone supplies no global trivialization of the ambient space, and a coordinate would provide exactly one (OpenAI 2026, §1).

The preprint's introduction supplies one locally nilpotent derivation, $D=h\partial_u-3u^2\partial_v+2u\partial_w$, annihilating $h,x,y,s$ and so $F$; its kernel, studied in Maubach (2000), is far larger than $\mathbb{C}[F]$. The content lies in two further derivations, commuting with $D$ and each other and independent over $R$, that cut the common kernel down to exactly $\mathbb{C}[F]$: $\supseteq$ in (v) follows from (i), while $\subseteq$ is a four-variable kernel computation. For (vi) the introduction indicates the mechanism: an identity in $R$ exhibits a critical point of $F$ on the fibre over $-1$, and a coordinate has nowhere-vanishing gradient by the Jacobian argument; the Lean statement needs this for every element of $\mathbb{C}[F_n]$. For $n>4$ the extras are prescribed, so the statement must be reduced to four variables across `rename (fourInclusion hn)`.

## Formalization scope

The target is the following declaration, published verbatim from openai/math commit adc7f12 in the default environment (Lean 4.33.1, Mathlib 0df444a) and referenced, not redrafted; the definition bundle `CommutingDerivations` (`S`, `P`, `R`, `h`, `u`, `v`, `w`, `x`, `y`, `s`, `p`, `F`, `fourInclusion`, `extendedF`, `blocksToAmbient`, `blocksToOutput`, `LocallyNilpotent`) is published the same way.

```lean
theorem commuting_derivations_with_ordinary_extras (n : ℕ) (hn : 4 ≤ n) :
    ∃ d : Fin (n-1) → Derivation ℂ (MvPolynomial (Fin n) ℂ) (MvPolynomial (Fin n) ℂ),
      (∀ i, d i (extendedF hn + 1) = 0) ∧
      (∀ i j p, d i (d j p) = d j (d i p)) ∧
      (∀ i, LocallyNilpotent (d i)) ∧
      LinearIndependent (MvPolynomial (Fin n) ℂ) d ∧
      (∀ p, (∀ i, d i p = 0) ↔
        p ∈ Algebra.adjoin ℂ ({extendedF hn} : Set (MvPolynomial (Fin n) ℂ))) ∧
      (∀ g, (∀ i, d i g = 0) →
        ¬ ∃ (e : MvPolynomial (Fin n) ℂ ≃ₐ[ℂ] MvPolynomial (Fin n) ℂ) (j : Fin n),
          e (X j) = g) ∧
      (∀ i : Fin (n-4), d (blocksToOutput hn (Sum.inl i)) =
        pderiv (blocksToAmbient hn (Sum.inl i))) := by
  sorry
```

Conventions:

- **Ring.** Everything is over $\mathbb{C}$ in `MvPolynomial (Fin n) ℂ`; $h,u,v,w$ are `X 0, …, X 3`. The `+ 1` is the ring unit; as `Derivation` sends $1$ to $0$, the first conjunct is equivalent to `d i (extendedF hn) = 0`.
- **Subtraction.** `n-1`, `n-4` are truncated, but exact under `hn`. For $n=4$, `Fin (n-4)` is empty, the last conjunct is vacuous and the family has three members.
- **Derivations.** `Derivation ℂ A A` bundles $\mathbb{C}$-linearity and Leibniz; commutation is pointwise. `LocallyNilpotent` uses `Function.iterate` with the exponent chosen after the element (exponent $0$ serves only the element $0$).
- **Linear independence** is over the polynomial ring, not over $\mathbb{C}$, via the module structure by post-multiplication. Since `Fin (n-1)` is nonempty, the zero family is excluded, so no trivializing choice of `d` exists.
- **Kernel.** The fifth conjunct is a biconditional for every `p`, hence both inclusions; `Algebra.adjoin ℂ {extendedF hn}` is $\mathbb{C}[F_n]$, constants included.
- **Coordinates.** The sixth conjunct denies, for every kernel element, being the image of some `X j` under a `≃ₐ[ℂ]` automorphism.
- **Extras.** `blocksToOutput hn (Sum.inl i)` has value $3+i$ and `blocksToAmbient hn (Sum.inl i)` value $4+i$, so the last conjunct reads $d_{3+i}=\partial_{4+i}$. No decidability or finiteness instances enter beyond `Fin`; the section is noncomputable.

Infrastructure needed: derivations on `MvPolynomial` from values on variables (`MvPolynomial.mkDerivation`), iterate and Leibniz lemmas for powers of a derivation, propagation of local nilpotency from generators, kernel computations, linear independence over a non-field, and transport across `MvPolynomial.rename`. Welcome contributions: a locally-nilpotent-derivation API; the four-variable core (a concrete triple on $R$); the kernel computation; the lemma that no element of $\mathbb{C}[F]$ is a coordinate; the $n>4$ transfer.
